SCIENTIFIC MOISTURE DEFENSE · 10-YEAR WARRANTY

Engineered for permanence.
Zero moisture passage.

Our certified technicians apply specialized primer, high-tensile membrane sheets, and conduct mandatory 72-hour standing water pond testing before handover.

STEP 01

Surface Prep

Mechanical grinding, cleaning, debris removal, and polyurethane crack injection.

STEP 02

Deep Primer

Deep-penetrating polymer bonding primer sealing microscopic capillary pores.

STEP 03

Membrane Laying

High-tensile elastomeric or torch-on bituminous membrane with double overlap fillets.

STEP 04

72-Hr Pond Test

Flooding treated slabs under continuous standing water for 72 hours with zero seepage.

STEP 05

Protective Screed

Polymer screed shielding the membrane from UV, thermal expansion, and mechanical wear.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS · KRISHNAGIRI

House Construction & Planning
Everything You Need To Know

Expert answers on house construction costs per sq.ft, 2D Vastu plans, 3D elevations, DTCP plan sanction approvals, and 10-year certified waterproofing in Krishnagiri.

How much does house construction cost per sq.ft in Krishnagiri? ▼
Turnkey residential house construction in Krishnagiri typically ranges from ₹2,000 to ₹2,400+ per sq. ft. based on your choice of specification level (Standard, Premium, or Luxury). Our packages encompass end-to-end architectural planning, RCC column casting, brick masonry, plumbing, joinery, and 10-year certified waterproofing. You can test your budget instantly using our built-in Construction Cost Estimator above.
Do you provide 100% Vastu-compliant 2D house plans? ▼
Yes. Every residential layout prepared by RAGAV BUILDERS adheres strictly to traditional Vastu principles tailored specifically for North, East, West, and South-facing plots (including standard 30x40, 30x50, 40x60 dimensions, 2BHK, 3BHK, and duplex floor plans) ensuring optimum natural ventilation, positive energy flow, and functional room ergonomics.
What is included in your 3D elevation and interior design services? ▼
Our 3D studio delivers photorealistic 3D front elevations showcasing modern exterior wall textures, balcony cantilever profiles, boundary gates, and nighttime architectural lighting. For interior designs, we provide turnkey execution of modular kitchens, designer false ceilings with ambient cove LED lighting, master bedroom wardrobes, custom TV wall units, and hand-carved pooja rooms.
Do you assist with building blueprints and DTCP / municipal approvals? ▼
Yes. We prepare complete municipal blueprint documentation, load calculation drawings, and structural stability certifications, liaising through the statutory DTCP and local panchayat building plan approval process across Krishnagiri district to secure quick sanctions.
How does your 10-Year certified waterproofing guarantee work? ▼
Every build is fortified with our certified 5-stage waterproofing defense applied across terrace slabs, bathrooms, sunken areas, and exterior walls. Prior to handover, we conduct a mandatory 72-hour standing water pond test to verify zero moisture seepage, accompanied by an official 10-year written warranty.
Which locations in and around Krishnagiri do you take up construction? ▼
From our headquarters at District Stadium Road, we take up turnkey residential and commercial projects across Krishnagiri Town, Bayanapalli, Wahab Nagar, New Pet, Bargur, Kaveripattinam, Pochampalli, Hosur, Uthangarai, Shoolagiri, Mathur, Veppanapalli, and Kelamangalam.
