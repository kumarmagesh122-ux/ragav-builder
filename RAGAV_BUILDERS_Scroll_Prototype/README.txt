RAGAV BUILDERS WEBSITE

HOW TO RUN
1. Open a terminal inside this folder.
2. Run: python -m http.server 8000
3. Visit: http://localhost:8000

BACKGROUND
The full-page canvas seeks assets/bgvideo_clean.mp4 according to page scroll position.
It starts on the first frame and progresses to the final frame by the Contact section.
Playback is scroll-controlled rather than autoplay. The video is drawn proportionally
with a dark navy overlay, and the gradient remains as a fallback if video loading fails.

INTERACTIVE SERVICES
The source video is 20.125 seconds at 1280x720. Its six continuous service ranges
follow the visible sequence of interior design (3.2-5.2s), site planning / Vastu
(5.2-7.4s), blueprint design (7.4-9.2s), 3D elevation (9.2-11.5s), construction
(11.5-15.8s), and protective-treatment work (15.8-17.0s). The opening and closing
brand cards are not assigned to a service. Selecting a card opens one
scroll-controlled canvas viewer for that service; scrolling down or up seeks forward
or backward within its independent range. Previous/Next and Back to All Services
remain available, and switching services resets the viewer to the new range's first
frame. The viewer loads only the shared source video on first selection instead of
preloading six separate image sequences. While it is open, the page background video
holds its first frame so it does not show a second moving service sequence.
